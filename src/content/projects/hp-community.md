- Built and run two live progressive web apps for a faith community: a companion app, [hpcommunity.app](https://hpcommunity.app) (about 25 weekly active users over three months, peaking at 38, tracked in PostHog), and a reading library, [read.hpcommunity.app](https://read.hpcommunity.app).
- The library serves 7,400+ paragraphs across two books with full-text search and annotation, plus reading plans whose daily target recalculates from each reader's actual progress.
